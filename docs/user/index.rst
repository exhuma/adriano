Users
=====

This section is for people who read and review decisions.
It explains, step by step, how to find a decision, understand its
state and, if you are a reviewer, accept or reject it.

New here?
Read :doc:`concepts` first; it takes two minutes and explains the
words used everywhere else.

What do you want to do?

Find a decision
   :doc:`searching` shows how to search, filter and share a link
   to your results.

Understand a decision page
   :doc:`reading-a-record` explains the banners, the status and the
   sidebar.
   :doc:`errata` covers corrections that were reported afterwards.

Accept or reject a decision
   :doc:`reviewing` walks through the review step by step.

Write a decision
   :doc:`writing-adrs` describes the file format.

Check that decisions are being collected
   :doc:`sources-page` is for operators and shows whether each
   source was read successfully.

Sign in or change the theme
   :doc:`signing-in` covers roles and the menu.
