Adriano
=======

Adriano is a catalog for architecture decision records (ADRs).
It collects the decisions your teams write down, wherever they keep
them, and shows them in one searchable place.

Decisions start out as candidates.
A reviewer accepts or rejects each one, and the catalog keeps the
history, so you can always see what was decided, by whom and when.
Once a decision is published, it stays easy to find and easy to
correct.

The point is trust: anyone can look up what was decided and why,
and can tell a settled decision from a proposal that is still
under review.
